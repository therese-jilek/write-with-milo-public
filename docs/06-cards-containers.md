# Cards and containers

Cards are used for genuinely bounded tools and repeated items, not as decoration around every section.

## Patterns

- White surfaces carry assignments and active writing.
- Cool page color separates the surrounding workspace from writing surfaces.
- Soft lavender identifies optional support choices.
- Borders are quiet and consistent.
- Nested cards are avoided when spacing can communicate hierarchy.
- Content areas reserve enough space for their expected states to prevent layout jumps.
