# Responsive behavior

Milo reflows content while preserving task order and control meaning.

## Wide screens

- Center the complete workspace.
- Keep writing lines at a readable length.
- Keep support tools in a stable adjacent column.

## Narrow screens

- Stack the writing area before writing support.
- Place progress after the active support content.
- Keep controls in normal document flow.
- Prevent horizontal page scrolling.

## Accessibility

- Support keyboard navigation and visible focus.
- Preserve content at browser zoom and narrow effective widths.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep important targets at least `44px`.
